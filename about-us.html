Ethiopia’s Trusted Security & Utility Solutions Partner

Ethiopia's trusted partner in advanced security technology. In a world where safety and security are paramount, we deliver peace of mind through state-of-the-art surveillance and security systems tailored to protect your home, business, and assets.

Based in the heart of Ethiopia, we specialize in the comprehensive supply, installation, and maintenance of CCTV security cameras and a wide array of electronic security technologies. We understand that every client has unique needs. That’s why our core strength lies in developing and implementing high-quality, cost-effective, and completely customized solutions. Our team of skilled professionals is dedicated to providing the most reliable security solution in Ethiopia, ensuring your safety is always in expert hands.

Your Safety, Our Mission

AtoZe Engineering is not just another security company. We specialize in crafting custom security strategies that are both effective and scalable. Whether it's CCTV camera installation, alarm system integration, or access control systems, our experts design solutions that protect what matters most — your people, property, and peace of mind.
